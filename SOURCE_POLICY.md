# Source Policy

Guidelines for research standards, attribution, evidence quality, and review rules when expanding the StudyVerse knowledge base.

## Research Standards
1. **Prioritize Authoritative Sources**: Use official documentation, established educational institutions, and reputable reference works.
2. **Primary vs. Secondary**: Prefer primary sources for original findings and authoritative references for established knowledge.
3. **Accuracy over Volume**: Do not fabricate citations, quotations, research papers, statistics, experiments, or benchmarks.

## Attribution & Evidence
1. **Explicit Citations**: Important factual claims must be supported by appropriate sources when external research is used.
2. **Context**: Distinguish between established consensus, active debate, hypotheses, and speculation.
3. **Source Recording**: Record source titles, publishers, URLs, relevant publication dates, and access dates where available.

## Review Rules
1. **Validation Pipeline**: All generated content must pass factual, source, security, and accessibility checks before publication.
2. **Human Override**: Complex, ambiguous, or high-stakes content requires human review.
3. **Freshness Checks**: Time-sensitive information must be flagged for periodic review.
