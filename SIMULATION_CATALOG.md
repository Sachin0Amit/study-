# Simulation Catalog

Descriptions of the interactive simulations available in the platform.

## Physics & Engineering
- **Projectile Playground (`projectile-lab`)**: Launch with/without air drag, scrub energy bars, adjust angles.
- **Kepler Orbit Simulator (`orbit-lab`)**: Drag planets, verify Kepler's laws, visualize swept areas.
- **Kinetic Theory Box (`gas-lab`)**: Particle collisions, Maxwell-Boltzmann histogram, adiabatic/isothermal processes.
- **Waves & Interference Studio (`wave-lab`)**: Standing harmonics, 2-source ripples, double-slit intensity.
- **Special Relativity Explorer (`relativity-lab`)**: Light clocks, spacetime diagrams, twin paradox.

## Electronics
- **Breadboard & CRT Oscilloscope Sim (`breadboard-oscilloscope-sim`)**: 30-column breadboard, function generator, dual-channel scope.
- **DC Circuit Sandbox (`dc-circuit-lab`)**: Build series/parallel networks, monitor current flow and power.
- **Thévenin & Norton Lab (`thevenin-lab`)**: Collapse linear networks to equivalents.
- **Bode & Filter Designer (`filter-lab`)**: Drag corner frequencies for RC, RLC, notch filters.

## Computer Science
- **Graph Playground (`graph-playground`)**: Build graphs, run BFS/DFS/Dijkstra, check Euler circuits.
- **CPU Scheduler (`cpu-scheduler`)**: Visualize FCFS, SJF, Round Robin scheduling.
- **Event Loop Lab (`event-loop-lab`)**: Watch the JS call stack, microtask queue, and timers drain.
- **Karnaugh Map Minimizer (`kmap-minimizer`)**: Drop minterms, find minimal SOP expressions.

## Chemistry
- **Periodic Table & Trends Lab (`periodic-table-lab`)**: Explore elements, electron shells, periodic trends.
- **VSEPR Molecular Geometry 3D (`molecular-lab`)**: Rotate 3D molecules, inspect hybridization and bond angles.
- **Chemical Kinetics (`kinetics-lab`)**: Simulate rate laws, half-lives, and Arrhenius activation energy.

*Refer to `src/components/interactives/registry.tsx` for the full, updated list.*
