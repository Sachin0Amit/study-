   219→# PART 2 — DRDO SCIENTIST 'B' RECRUITMENT (RAC — Recruitment & Assessment Centre)
   220→
   221→## 0. IMPORTANT: Evolution of the selection pattern (verified from official ads)
   222→
   223→DRDO Scientist 'B' recruitment is run by RAC (rac.gov.in), DRDO's recruitment body. The pattern has changed over the years — a study platform should reflect all of these:
   224→
   225→| Era / Advt | Selection route | Key facts (official) |
   226→|---|---|---|
   227→| **Legacy "SET" era ads** (e.g., RAC Advt 118/2016, 129/2018 — and the classic **Tier I + Tier II** pattern requested for this platform) | **Tier-I (screening) → Tier-II (descriptive) → Interview** | Tier-I: 100-mark screening test (Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning & Problem Solving, General Awareness, English Language, General Science — ~20 questions each); Tier-II: 75-mark descriptive paper — **60 marks engineering discipline + 15 marks "Research Aptitude"** (Science & Technology priorities, Research aptitude, Quantitative aptitude & reasoning); then Personal Interview |
   228→| **Advt 140 (2022, 630 vacancies)** | Part-I disciplines (**ECE, ME, CSE**): GATE shortlist (1:25) → **Written Examination** → Interview (1:5). Part-II disciplines (**EE** + 14 others): GATE → Interview | Written exam: **2 descriptive papers of 300 marks each, 3 hours each** (600 total, offline paper-based), syllabus **similar to UPSC Engineering Services (ESE) Main in the relevant discipline** (downloadable from RAC). Final merit: **80% weightage of written exam + 20% interview** (Part-II: 80% GATE + 20% interview). Interview qualifying marks: **70% (UR), 60% (EWS/OBC/SC/ST/PwD)** |
   229→| **Advt 156 (current, 2025)** | **Valid GATE score (2023/2024/2025) + Personal Interview** for all disciplines | Final merit: **80% weightage of GATE score + 20% weightage of interview**; interview qualifying 70% UR / 60% reserved. No written exam |
   230→| **Related: CEPTAM (DRDO's other recruitment body)** | STA-'B' (Senior Technical Assistant): Tier-I CBT + Tier-II CBT | Tier-I: 150 Q / 150 marks / 120 min — General Science (40), General English (30), Quantitative Aptitude/General Maths (40), General Intelligence & Reasoning (40); **negative marking 0.25**; qualifying 40% UR/OBC/EWS, 35% SC/ST/PwBD. Tier-II: 20-min Personality test (qualifying) + **subject-specific CBT: 120 Q / 120 marks / 120 min**, negative 0.25. (Tech-'A' adds a Trade Test.) |
   231→
   232→**Disciplines that exist (beyond CSE/ECE/EE/ME):** Advt 140/156 recruit (or recruited) in **Electronics & Communication, Mechanical, Computer Science & Engg, Electrical, Material Science/Metallurgical, Physics, Chemistry, Chemical Engg, Aeronautical/Aerospace Engg, Mathematics, Civil Engg, Instrumentation Engg, Material Science, Naval Architecture, Environmental Science & Engg, Atmospheric Science, Microbiology, Biochemistry** (DRDO/ADA/DST/CME/WESEE etc.). CEPTAM STA-'B' subject syllabus additionally covers **Automobile Engg, Geology, Library Science, Psychology**. For the requested deep-dive, full Tier-II discipline syllabi are given below for **CSE, ECE, EE, ME**.
   233→
   234→## 1. Tier I Syllabus — Screening Test (legacy written-exam pattern; mirrors RAC/CEPTAM screening stage)
   235→
   236→**Pattern (legacy RAC Tier-I):** 100 marks, ~2 hours, objective/screening in nature, **negative marking 0.25 per wrong answer**; used to shortlist candidates for Tier-II. Five sections of ~20 questions each:
   237→
   238→- **Section A — Quantitative Aptitude (≈20 Q)**
   239→  - Number system, HCF & LCM, surds/indices
   240→  - Percentages; profit & loss; discount; simple & compound interest
   241→  - Ratio & proportion; mixtures & alligation; partnership; averages
   242→  - Time & work; pipes & cisterns; time, speed & distance; trains/boats
   243→  - Algebra basics (linear & quadratic equations, polynomials)
   244→  - Geometry & mensuration basics; trigonometry basics
   245→  - Elementary statistics & data interpretation (tables, charts, graphs)
   246→- **Section B — Reasoning & Problem Solving (≈20 Q)**
   247→  - Analogies (word/number/letter); classification (odd one out)
   248→  - Series (number, letter, figure); coding–decoding
   249→  - Blood relations; direction & distance sense
   250→  - Syllogisms; statement–assumption/conclusion; logical deduction
   251→  - Seating arrangements & puzzles; Venn diagrams
   252→  - Order & ranking; calendar & clock problems; non-verbal/figure reasoning
   253→- **Section C — General Awareness (≈20 Q)**
   254→  - Current affairs (national & international)
   255→  - Indian history, polity & constitution, geography, economy
   256→  - Science & technology in news; defence & security developments, DRDO missions/products
   257→  - Awards, honours, sports; books & authors; important days; organizations (UN, ISRO, DRDO, HAL, BARC…)
   258→- **Section D — English Language (≈20 Q)**
   259→  - Grammar: articles, prepositions, tenses, subject–verb agreement, error spotting
   260→  - Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms & phrases
   261→  - Sentence completion/jumbled sentences; active–passive & direct–indirect (basic)
   262→  - Reading comprehension (short passages)
   263→- **Section E — General Science (≈20 Q)**
   264→  - Physics (10+2 level: mechanics, heat, optics, electricity, modern physics basics)
   265→  - Chemistry (10+2 level: atomic structure, bonding, acids/bases, metals/non-metals, carbon compounds basics)
   266→  - Biology (cells, genetics basics, human physiology, plant biology, ecology)
   267→  - Everyday science; recent S&T developments; space & defence science
   268→
   269→*(Cross-check: the current CEPTAM Tier-I uses the same four/five families of sections — General Science, General English, Quantitative Aptitude/General Maths, General Intelligence & Reasoning — confirming the section composition.)*

   271→## 2. Tier II — Discipline Syllabi (descriptive papers; per official RAC descriptive syllabus ≈ UPSC ESE-Mains style, as documented for Advt 140 written exam)
   272→
   273→> The 2022 RAC written exam consisted of **Paper 1 (300 marks, 3 h)** and **Paper 2 (300 marks, 3 h)**, descriptive. The topic lists below are the official discipline syllabi (as published by RAC and reproduced by Testbook/Adda247/Embibe).
   274→
   275→### 2.1 Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
   276→
   277→**Paper 1 (CSE):**
   278→- **Engineering Mathematics**
   279→  - *Linear Algebra:* Matrices, determinants, system of linear equations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, LU decomposition, orthonormal bases; Transforms — Fourier, DCT, wavelets
   280→  - *Calculus:* Limits, continuity, differentiability, maxima and minima, mean value theorem, integration; Vector calculus — gradient, divergence, curl, integral theorems
   281→  - *Probability:* Random variables; probability distributions — uniform, normal, exponential, Poisson, binomial; mean, median, mode, standard deviation; conditional probability and Bayes theorem; Information theory: Shannon entropy, information and conditional information
   282→- **Discrete Mathematics:** Propositional and first-order logic; sets, relations, functions, partial orders and lattices, groups; graphs — connectivity, matching, colouring; combinatorics — counting, recurrence relations, generating functions
   283→- **Digital Logic:** Boolean algebra; combinational and sequential circuits; minimization; number representation and computer arithmetic (fixed and floating point)
   284→- **Computer Organization and Architecture:** Machine instructions and addressing modes; assembly language; ALU, data path and control unit; instruction pipelining; memory hierarchy — cache, main memory, secondary storage; I/O interface (interrupt and DMA modes); RISC, CISC, parallel architectures
   285→- **Programming and Data Structures:** Programming in C — functions, structures, memory management, recursion; arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, binary search trees, binary heaps, graphs; object-oriented programming basics
   286→- **Algorithms:** Searching, sorting, hashing; asymptotic worst-case time and space complexity; algorithm design techniques — greedy, dynamic programming, divide-and-conquer; graph search, minimum spanning trees, shortest paths
   287→
   288→**Paper 2 (CSE):**
   289→- **Compiler Design:** Lexical analysis, parsing, syntax-directed translation; runtime environments, intermediate code generation
   290→- **Operating System:** Processes, threads, inter-process communication, concurrency and synchronization, deadlocks; CPU scheduling; memory management and virtual memory; file systems; virtual machines and hypervisors; secure operating systems
   291→- **Theory of Computation:** Regular expressions and finite automata; context-free grammars and push-down automata; regular and context-free languages, pumping lemma; Turing machines and undecidability
   292→- **Databases:** ER model; relational model — relational algebra, tuple calculus, SQL; integrity constraints; normal forms; file organization, indexing (e.g., B and B+ trees); transactions and concurrency control
   293→- **Computer Networks:** Concept of layering, OSI model; LAN technologies (Ethernet); flow and error control techniques, switching; IPv4/IPv6, routers, routing algorithms (distance vector, link state); TCP/UDP and sockets, congestion control; application layer protocols (DNS, SMTP, POP, FTP, HTTP); basics of WiFi
   294→- **Security of Cyber Systems:** Cybersecurity principles; Kerberos, RADIUS; secure protocols (IPSec, SSH, SSL); basics of public and private key cryptography, hash functions, blockchains, digital signatures and certificates; firewalls, intrusion detection systems; basics of IoT security and cloud security; threats, vulnerabilities, malicious software; attacks — buffer overflow, DoS, DDoS, SQL injection, web application attacks
   295→- **AI and Machine Learning:** Classification and clustering techniques; dimensionality reduction — PCA, SVD; correlation and regression analysis; training and test data, overfitting, underfitting; search; ontology; decision trees; artificial neural networks — activation function, multi-layer perceptron, deep learning; applications of ANNs
   296→
   297→### 2.2 Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
   298→
   299→**Paper 1 (ECE):**
   300→- **Basic Electronics Engineering:** Basics of semiconductors; diode/transistor basics and characteristics; diodes for different uses; junction & field-effect transistors (BJTs, JFETs, MOSFETs); transistor amplifiers of different types, oscillators and other circuits; basics of integrated circuits (ICs); bipolar, MOS and CMOS ICs; basics of linear ICs, operational amplifiers and their applications — linear/non-linear; optical sources/detectors; basics of optoelectronics and its applications
   301→- **Basic Electrical Engineering:** DC circuits — Ohm's & Kirchhoff's laws, mesh and nodal analysis, circuit theorems; electromagnetism, Faraday's & Lenz's laws, induced EMF and its uses; single-phase AC circuits; transformers, efficiency; basics of DC machines, induction machines, synchronous machines; electrical power sources — hydroelectric, thermal, nuclear, wind, solar; basics of batteries and their uses
   302→- **Materials Science:** Electrical engineering materials; crystal structure & defects; ceramic materials — structures, composites, processing and uses; insulating laminates for electronics; magnetic materials — classification, ferrites, ferro/para-magnetic materials; nano materials — preparation, purification, sintering, nanoparticles and uses; nano-optical/magnetic/electronic materials; superconductivity and uses
   303→- **Electronic Measurements and Instrumentation:** Principles of measurement, accuracy, precision and standards; analogue and digital systems for measurement; measuring instruments for different applications; static/dynamic characteristics of measurement systems, errors, statistical analysis and curve fitting; measurement of non-electrical quantities; basics of telemetry; transducers and displays; data acquisition system basics
   304→- **Network Theory:** Network graphs & matrices; wye–delta transformation; linear constant-coefficient differential equations — time-domain analysis of RLC circuits; solution of network equations using Laplace transforms — frequency-domain analysis of RLC circuits; 2-port network parameters, driving-point & transfer functions; state equations for networks; steady-state sinusoidal analysis
   305→- **Analog and Digital Circuits:** Small-signal equivalent circuits of diodes, BJTs and FETs; diode circuits for different uses; biasing & stability of BJT & JFET amplifier circuits; analysis/design of single/multi-stage amplifiers; feedback and uses; active filters, timers, multipliers, waveshaping, A/D–D/A converters; Boolean algebra and uses; logic gates, digital IC families, combinational/sequential circuits; multiplexers, counters/registers/memories/microprocessors — design & applications
   306→
   307→**Paper 2 (ECE):**
   308→- **Analog and Digital Communication Systems:** Random signals, noise, probability theory, information theory; analog vs digital communication & applications; AM, FM systems — transmitters/receivers, theory/practice/standards, SNR comparison; digital communication basics — sampling, quantizing, coding, PCM, DPCM, multiplexing (audio/video); digital modulation — ASK, FSK, PSK; multiple access — TDMA, FDMA, CDMA; optical communication — fiber optics, theory, practice/standards
   309→- **Control Systems:** Classification of signals and systems; application of signal and system theory; system realization; transforms & their applications; signal flow graphs, Routh–Hurwitz criterion, root loci, Nyquist/Bode plots; feedback systems — open & closed loop, stability analysis; steady-state, transient and frequency-response analysis; design of control systems, compensators, lead/lag compensation, PID and industrial controllers
   310→- **Computer Organization and Architecture (ECE flavor):** Basic architecture, CPU, I/O organization, memory organization, peripheral devices, trends; hardware/software issues; data representation & programming; operating systems basics — processes, memory management, virtual memory, file systems, protection & security; databases — types, characteristics, design; transactions and concurrency control; elements of programming languages
   311→- **Electromagnetics:** Elements of vector calculus; Maxwell's equations — basic concepts; Gauss' and Stokes' theorems; wave propagation through different media; transmission lines — types, basics, Smith chart, impedance matching/transformation, S-parameters, pulse excitation; waveguides — rectangular types, modes, cut-off frequency, dispersion, dielectric types; antennas — radiation pattern, monopoles/dipoles, gain, active/passive arrays
   312→- **Advanced Electronics Topics:** VLSI technology — processing, lithography, interconnects, packaging, testing; VLSI design — principles, MUX/ROM/PLA-based design, Moore & Mealy circuit design; pipeline concepts & functions; design for testability; DSP — discrete-time signals/systems; digital filters — FIR/IIR types, design, speech/audio/radar signal processing uses; microprocessors & microcontrollers — basics, interrupts, DMA, instruction sets, interfacing; embedded systems
   313→- **Advanced Communication Topics:** Communication networks — principles/practices/technologies/OSI model/security; basic packet multiplexed streams/scheduling; cellular networks — types, analysis, protocols (TCP/TCPIP); microwave & satellite communication — terrestrial/space LOS systems, link calculations, system design; communication satellites — orbits, characteristics, systems; fibre optic communication systems — block schematics, link calculations, system design

   315→### 2.3 Electrical Engineering (EE)
   316→
   317→> Note: In Advt 140 (2022), EE recruitment was via **GATE + interview** (Part-II), so no RAC descriptive paper was published for EE that cycle. The EE discipline syllabus below is compiled from (a) the legacy RAC/SET Tier-II EE scope (GATE-EE/ESE-EE-equivalent subjects: electric circuits, fields, machines, power systems, power electronics & drives, measurements, control), and (b) DRDO's own official **CEPTAM STA-'B' Tier-II syllabus for Electrical Engineering** — so it is representative of what a DRDO EE paper tests.
   318→
   319→**Core areas (Tier-II EE):**
   320→- **Engineering Mathematics** (common): Linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, probability & statistics, numerical methods
   321→- **Electric Circuits & Circuit Analysis**
   322→  - DC circuits: Ohm's & Kirchhoff's laws, mesh/nodal analysis, network theorems (Thevenin, Norton, superposition, max power transfer), star/delta transformations
   323→  - AC circuits: single- and three-phase phasor analysis, resonance, power factor, power in AC circuits
   324→  - Transients (RL, RC, RLC); Laplace-domain network analysis; two-port networks; coupled circuits and mutual inductance; network graphs & state equations
   325→- **Electricity & Magnetism / Electromagnetic Fields**
   326→  - Electrostatics & magnetostatics; Maxwell's equations; Faraday's & Lenz's laws; induced EMF; inductance/capacitance; electromagnetic force; field theory applications in machines
   327→- **Electrical Machines**
   328→  - DC machines — generators/motors, characteristics, starting & speed control, commutation
   329→  - Transformers — construction, equivalent circuit, losses, efficiency, regulation, auto-transformers, three-phase connections, parallel operation
   330→  - Induction machines — slip-torque characteristics, starting & speed control, single-phase motors
   331→  - Synchronous machines — EMF, armature reaction, voltage regulation, synchronizing, power circles; special machines (stepper, BLDC, servo basics)
   332→- **Electrical Power Engineering (Generation, Transmission & Distribution)**
   333→  - Generation: thermal, hydro, nuclear, wind, solar PV basics; economics of generation, load curves, tariffs
   334→  - Transmission: line parameters & models, performance, HVDC vs HVAC basics, insulators, corona, cables; substations; voltage regulation; load flow (basics), symmetrical components, fault analysis, symmetrical/unsymmetrical faults; switchgear & protection (relays, circuit breakers, CTs/PTs, protection schemes); power system stability basics; distribution systems & earthing; utilization of electrical energy (illumination, traction, drives, heating)
   335→- **Power Electronics & Drives (Industrial Electronics & Control)**
   336→  - Power semiconductor devices (diode, BJT, MOSFET, IGBT, thyristor, TRIAC); rectifiers (controlled/uncontrolled), choppers, inverters, cycloconverters, AC voltage controllers; switching techniques; electric drives — DC & AC motor control, speed control methods; converters applications
   337→- **Control Systems**
   338→  - Mathematical modelling of physical systems; transfer functions; block diagrams & signal flow graphs; time response; stability — Routh–Hurwitz, root locus, Bode/Nyquist; compensators & PID/industrial controllers; state-space basics
   339→- **Measurements & Instrumentation (Electrical and Electronics Instrumentation)**
   340→  - Measurement principles, accuracy/precision/standards; static/dynamic characteristics, errors; measuring instruments — PMMC, moving iron, dynamometer wattmeters, energy meters; instrument transformers; bridges (Wheatstone, Kelvin, Maxwell, Schering); measurement of R/L/C, power, energy, power factor; digital measurement (DVMs, counters), transducers, displays, data acquisition; telemetry basics; oscilloscopes
   341→- **Electronics & Microprocessors (EE-flavor)**
   342→  - Electronic components & devices; analog electronics basics (amplifiers, op-amps, oscillators, filters); digital electronics — Boolean algebra, gates, combinational/sequential circuits, logic families, ADC/DAC; microprocessors (8085) & microcontrollers (8051) — architecture, programming, interfacing, peripherals; embedded systems basics
   343→- **Electrical & Electronics Materials**
   344→  - Conducting, insulating & magnetic materials; crystal structure & defects; superconductivity; dielectric materials; nano-materials basics; batteries & energy storage
   345→- **Estimation & Costing / Electrical Drawing / Maintenance & Repair**
   346→  - Wiring systems, estimating & costing of installations; reading electrical schematics; maintenance practices of electrical & electronic equipment
   347→- **Signal & Systems basics (for EEE-inclusive variants):** continuous/discrete signals, sampling, Fourier/Laplace transforms applications
   348→
   349→*(Official CEPTAM STA-'B' EE topic list, verbatim, for cross-reference: "Electrical and Electronics Materials, Electricity and Magnetism, Electrical Engineering Drawing, Estimation & Costing, Electrical Circuit Analysis, Electrical Machines, Electrical Power Engineering, Instrumentation & Control Engineering and Electronics & Micro Processor and relevant topic from subject".    351→### 2.4 Mechanical Engineering (ME)
   352→
   353→**Paper 1 (ME):**
   354→- **Fluid Mechanics:** Basic concepts and properties of fluids, manometry, fluid statics, buoyancy, equations of motion, Bernoulli's equation and applications, viscous flow of incompressible fluids, laminar and turbulent flows, flow through pipes and head losses in pipes
   355→- **Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer:** Thermodynamic systems and processes; properties of pure substance; Zeroth, First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics; entropy, irreversibility and availability; analysis of thermodynamic cycles related to energy conversion — Rankine, Otto, Diesel and Dual cycles; ideal and real gases; compressibility factor; gas mixtures; modes of heat transfer, steady and unsteady heat conduction, thermal resistance, fins, free and forced convection, correlations for convective heat transfer, radiative heat transfer — radiation heat transfer coefficient, boiling and condensation, heat exchanger performance analysis
   356→- **IC Engines, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning:** SI and CI engines, engine systems and components, performance characteristics and testing of IC engines; fuels; emissions and emission control; vapour compression refrigeration, refrigerants and working cycles, compressors, condensers, evaporators and expansion devices; other refrigeration systems — vapour absorption, vapour jet, thermoelectric, vortex tube; psychrometric properties and processes, comfort chart, comfort and industrial air conditioning, load calculations and heat pumps
   357→- **Turbo Machinery:** Reciprocating and rotary pumps; Pelton wheel, Kaplan and Francis turbines, velocity diagrams, impulse and reaction principles; steam and gas turbines; theory of jet propulsion — pulse jet and ram jet engines; reciprocating and rotary compressors — theory and applications
   358→- **Power Plant Engineering:** Rankine and Brayton cycles with regeneration and reheat; fuels and their properties, flue gas analysis; boilers, steam turbines, and other power plant components — condensers, air ejectors, electrostatic precipitators, cooling towers — theory, design, types and applications
   359→- **Renewable Sources of Energy:** Solar radiation; solar thermal energy collection — flat plate and focusing collectors, materials and performance; solar thermal energy storage; applications — heating, cooling and power generation; solar photovoltaic conversion; harnessing of wind energy, biomass and tidal energy — methods and applications; working principles of fuel cells
   360→
   361→**Paper 2 (ME):**
   362→- **Engineering Mechanics (Mechanics of Materials/Solid Mechanics):** Analysis of system of forces, friction, centroid and centre of gravity, dynamics; stresses and strains — compound stresses and strains; bending moment and shear force diagrams; theory of bending stresses — slope and deflection — torsion; thin and thick cylinders, spheres
   363→- **Engineering Materials:** Basic crystallography; alloys and phase diagrams; heat treatment; ferrous and non-ferrous metals; non-metallic materials; basics of nano-materials; mechanical properties and testing; corrosion prevention and control
   364→- **Mechanisms and Machines:** Types of kinematic pairs, mobility, inversions, kinematic analysis; velocity and acceleration analysis of planar mechanisms; cams with uniform acceleration and retardation, cycloidal motion, oscillating followers; vibrations — free and forced vibration of undamped and damped SDOF systems, transmissibility ratio, vibration isolation, critical speed of shafts; gears — geometry of tooth profiles, law of gearing, involute profile, interference, helical, spiral and worm gears; gear trains — simple, compound and epicyclic; dynamic analysis — slider–crank mechanisms, turning moment computations, balancing of revolving & reciprocating masses; gyroscopes — effect of gyroscopic couple on automobiles, ships and aircraft; governors
   365→- **Design of Machine Elements:** Design for static and dynamic loading; failure theories; fatigue strength and the S–N diagram; design of machine elements — riveted, welded and bolted joints; shafts; spur gears; rolling and sliding contact bearings; brakes and clutches; flywheels
   366→- **Manufacturing, Industrial and Maintenance Engineering:** Metal casting, metal forming, metal joining, machining and machine tool operations; limits, fits and tolerances; metrology and inspection; computer-integrated manufacturing, FMS; production planning and control; inventory control and operations research — CPM–PERT; failure concepts and characteristics — reliability, failure analysis; machine vibration, data acquisition, fault detection, vibration monitoring, field balancing of rotors, noise monitoring, wear and debris analysis, signature analysis; NDT techniques in condition monitoring
   367→- **Mechatronics and Robotics:** Microprocessors and microcontrollers — architecture, programming, I/O, computer interfacing, programmable logic controller (PLC); sensors and actuators — piezoelectric accelerometer, Hall-effect sensor, optical encoder, resolver, Inductosyn, pneumatic and hydraulic actuators, stepper motor; control systems — mathematical modelling of physical systems, control signals, controllability and observability; robotics — robot classification, robot specification, notation; direct and inverse kinematics; homogeneous coordinates and arm equation of four-axis SCARA robot
   368→
   369→*(Cross-reference — official CEPTAM STA-'B' ME Tier-II topics: "Applied Mathematics, Engineering Drawing and CAD/CAM/CIM, Solid Mechanics and Machine Design, Fluid Mechanics and Hydraulic Machines, Thermal and Heat Transfer and Refrigeration & Air-conditioning, IC Engines, Engineering materials, Production Engineering and Industrial Engineering and relevant topic from subject".    387→## 3. DRDO Scientist B — Exam Pattern Summary (all routes)
   388→
   389→**Legacy Tier-I + Tier-II route (the structure requested for the platform):**
   390→- **Tier-I (Screening):** objective test, ~100 marks, ~2 hours; 5 sections (~20 questions each): Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning & Problem Solving, General Awareness, English Language, General Science; **negative marking 0.25 per wrong answer**; screening/qualifying in nature — used to shortlist for Tier-II
   391→- **Tier-II (Descriptive):** ~3 hours, **75 marks** = **60 marks engineering-discipline** descriptive questions + **15 marks** research aptitude (science & technology priorities, research aptitude, quantitative aptitude & reasoning)
   392→- **Tier-III — Personal Interview:** final stage; in later RAC ads the final merit used **80% weightage of written (Tier-II/descriptive) + 20% weightage of interview**; interviews require minimum qualifying marks — **70% (UR) / 60% (EWS/OBC/SC/ST/PwD)** (per official Advt 140/156 criteria)
   393→- Eligibility essentials: first-class B.E./B.Tech (or M.Sc. for science posts) in the advertised discipline + **valid GATE score** (or top IIT/NIT marks ≥80% in some ads); age limit ≤28 years (UR, DRDO) with category relaxations (OBC 31, SC/ST 33 etc.)
   394→
   395→**Advt 140 (2022) written-exam route (ECE/ME/CSE):** GATE-based shortlisting (1:25) → offline **descriptive written exam: 2 papers × 300 marks, 3 hours each (600 total)**, syllabus per UPSC ESE (Mains) in the discipline → interview (shortlist 1:5 from written merit) → final merit = 80% written + 20% interview; interview qualifying 70%/60%.
   396→
   397→**Current route (Advt 156, 2025):** shortlisting purely on **valid GATE score (2023/2024/2025)** → **Personal Interview** → final merit = **80% GATE + 20% interview**; interview qualifying 70% UR / 60% reserved. No negative marking concept (no CBT); medical examination & character-antecedent verification precede appointment (Level-10, 7th CPC pay matrix, basic pay ₹56,100).
   398→
   399→**CEPTAM STA-'B' (comparison):** Tier-I CBT 150 Q/150 marks/120 min (negative 0.25; qualifying 40%/35%) + Tier-II subject CBT 120 Q/120 marks/120 min (negative 0.25) + 20-minute personality test (qualifying).
   400→
   401→**Other DRDO scientist recruitment channels (context):** ROSSA (Registration of Students with Scholarships and Aptitude — for high-GATE/merit fresh candidates), campus/Institute-level selection for young scientists, lateral entry for experienced professionals, and DRDO Research Fellowships (JRF/SRF/RA) at laboratories.
