  1228→## JEE Advanced Exam Pattern (2025/2026)
  1229→
  1230→### Overall structure
  1231→| Attribute | Detail |
  1232→|---|---|
  1233→| Papers | **Two compulsory papers** — Paper 1 and Paper 2, held on the same day (typically 09:00–12:00 and 14:30–17:30) |
  1234→| Duration | 3 hours each (compensatory time for PwD candidates) |
  1235→| Mode | Computer Based Test (CBT); English and Hindi |
  1236→| Sections | Each paper has 3 parts — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics — each with 4 sections |
  1237→| Question types | Single-correct MCQ; one-or-more-correct MCQ (partial marking); numerical answer type (NAT); matching-list; paragraph/comprehension-linked ("question stem") questions |
  1238→| Eligibility | Top ~2,50,000 (all categories included) in JEE Main Paper 1; max 2 attempts in 2 consecutive years; Class XII with ≥75% (65% reserved) or top-20 percentile; age criterion (born on or after 1 Oct 2001 for GE/OBC in 2026, 5-year relaxation for SC/ST/PwD) |
  1239→
  1240→### JEE Advanced 2026 Paper 1 (from official question paper) — 48 questions, 180 marks
  1241→Per subject (Physics / Chemistry / Maths) — 16 questions, 60 marks:
  1242→- **Section 1 (12 marks):** 4 single-correct MCQs — **+3** correct / 0 unanswered / **−1** incorrect
  1243→- **Section 2 (16 marks):** 4 one-or-more-correct MCQs — **+4** if all correct chosen; **partial +3** (all 4 correct but only 3 chosen), **+2** (≥3 correct, only 2 chosen, both correct), **+1** (≥2 correct, only 1 chosen and correct); 0 unanswered; **−1** otherwise
  1244→- **Section 3 (16 marks):** 4 numerical-value questions — **+4** correct / 0 otherwise (**no negative marking**)
  1245→- **Section 4 (16 marks):** 4 matching-list sets — **+4** correct option / 0 unanswered / **−1** otherwise
  1246→
  1247→### JEE Advanced 2026 Paper 2 (from official question paper) — 54 questions, 180 marks
  1248→Per subject — 18 questions, 60 marks:
  1249→- **Section 1 (12 marks):** 4 single-correct MCQs — **+3** / 0 / **−1**
  1250→- **Section 2 (20 marks):** 5 one-or-more-correct MCQs — same partial marking scheme as above (+4/+3/+2/+1/0/−1)
  1251→- **Section 3 (20 marks):** 5 numerical-value questions — **+4** / 0 (**no negative marking**)
  1252→- **Section 4 (8 marks):** 2 question stems, each with **2 linked numerical questions** — **+2** per correct numerical / 0 otherwise (no negative marking)
  1253→
  1254→> Combined 2026: 102 questions, 360 marks. **Note:** the exact section/question mix changes slightly every year (e.g., Paper 1 had 48 vs Paper 2's 54 questions in 2026), but question types and marking philosophy (partial marking on multi-correct, no negative on NAT) have been stable.
  1255→
  1256→### AAT — Architecture Aptitude Test (B.Arch at IITs)
  1257→- Only candidates who qualify JEE Advanced may register; result is qualifying/non-qualifying (no rank; B.Arch seats at IIT Roorkee, IIT Kharagpur, IIT (BHU) Varanasi)
  1258→- 3-hour pen-and-paper test; official syllabus sections:
  1259→  - **Freehand drawing** — simple drawing depicting the total object in its right form and proportion, surface texture, relative location and details of component parts in appropriate scale; common domestic/day-to-day usable objects (furniture, equipment, etc.) from memory
  1260→  - **Geometrical drawing** — exercises with lines, angles, triangles, quadrilaterals, polygons, circles; study of plan (top view), elevation (front/side views) of simple solid objects (prisms, cones, cylinders, cubes, splayed surface holders, etc.)
  1261→  - **Three-dimensional perception** — understanding and appreciation of 3D forms with building elements, colour, volume and orientation; visualisation through structuring objects in memory
  1262→  - **Imagination and aesthetic sensitivity** — composition exercise with given elements; context mapping; creativity check through innovative uncommon tests with familiar objects; sense of colour grouping or application
  1263→  - **Architectural awareness** — general interest and awareness of famous architectural creations (national and international), places and personalities (architects, designers, etc.)
