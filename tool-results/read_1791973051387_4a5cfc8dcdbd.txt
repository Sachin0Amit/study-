    19→## 1.1 Exam Pattern (official, GATE 2026 IIT Guwahati)
    20→
    21→| Particular | Detail |
    22→|---|---|
    23→| Mode | Computer Based Test (CBT), English only |
    24→| Duration | 3 hours (180 minutes) |
    25→| Total questions | **65** (10 General Aptitude + 55 subject questions incl. Engineering Mathematics) |
    26→| Total marks | **100** |
    27→| Marks split (CS paper) | **GA: 15 marks · Engineering Mathematics: 13 marks · Core Subject: 72 marks** |
    28→| GA question split | 10 questions — 5 × 1 mark + 5 × 2 marks |
    29→| Question types | (a) MCQ — 4 choices, 1 correct; (b) MSQ — multiple select; (c) NAT — Numerical Answer Type |
    30→| Marks per question | 1 mark or 2 marks |
    31→| Negative marking | **MCQ only:** 1/3 deducted per wrong 1-mark MCQ; 2/3 deducted per wrong 2-mark MCQ. **No negative marking for MSQ / NAT. No partial marking in MSQ.** |
    32→| Abilities tested | Recall, Comprehension, Application, Analysis & Synthesis |
    33→| Note | GATE 2026 conducted for 30 test papers; CS can be combined with a second paper per the two-paper combination list |

    37→## 1.2 General Aptitude (GA) — common to all papers (15 marks)
    38→
    39→- **Section 1: Verbal Aptitude**
    40→  - Basic English grammar: tenses, articles, adjectives, prepositions, conjunctions, verb-noun agreement, other parts of speech
    41→  - Basic vocabulary: words, idioms, and phrases in context
    42→  - Reading and comprehension; narrative sequencing
    43→- **Section 2: Quantitative Aptitude**
    44→  - Data interpretation: data graphs (bar graphs, pie charts, other graphs), 2- and 3-dimensional plots, maps, tables
    45→  - Numerical computation and estimation: ratios, percentages, powers, exponents and logarithms, permutations and combinations, series
    46→  - Mensuration and geometry; elementary statistics and probability
    47→- **Section 3: Analytical Aptitude**
    48→  - Logic: deduction and induction; analogy; numerical relations and reasoning
    49→- **Section 4: Spatial Aptitude**
    50→  - Transformation of shapes: translation, rotation, scaling, mirroring, assembling, grouping; paper folding, cutting, patterns in 2 and 3 dimensions
