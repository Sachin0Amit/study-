# Curriculum Map

This document outlines the subject hierarchy, learning levels, and major coverage gaps within StudyVerse.

## Subject Hierarchy

Currently, the platform organizes content into 37 main subject tracks (books) mapping to disciplines across mathematics, sciences, engineering, and arts.

### Mathematics & Sciences
- `math-master`: The Mathematics Master Library
- `phys-master`: The Physics Master Library
- `chem-master`: The Chemistry Master Library

### Foundation & Aptitude
- `english-aptitude`: English Language & Grammar
- `aptitude-reasoning`: Quantitative Aptitude & Logical Reasoning

### Computing & Software
- `programming-c-cpp`: Programming in C & C++
- `programming-java`: Programming in Java
- `web-development`: Web Technologies & Development
- `dsa-algorithms`: Data Structures & Algorithms
- `os-design`: Operating Systems
- `dbms-design`: Database Management Systems
- `computer-networks`: Computer Networks
- `software-emerging`: Software Engineering & Emerging Tech
- `cybersecurity`: Cybersecurity & Forensics
- `compiler-design`: Compiler Design
- `toc-computation`: Theory of Computation
- `ai-data-science`: Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
- `visual-computing`: Visual Computing & Graphics

### Electrical & Electronics
- `digital-electronics`: Digital Electronics & Logic Design
- `electronic-devices-circuits`: Electronic Devices & Circuits
- `signals-systems`: Signals & Systems
- `communication-systems`: Communication Systems
- `electromagnetics`: Engineering Electromagnetics
- `electric-circuits`: Electric Circuits & Network Analysis
- `electrical-machines`: Electrical Machines
- `power-systems`: Power Systems
- `microprocessors-embedded`: Microprocessors & Embedded Systems
- `vlsi-design`: VLSI Design & Nanotechnology

### Mechanical & Industrial
- `mechanics-graphics`: Engineering Mechanics & Graphics
- `solids-materials`: Mechanics of Solids & Materials
- `machines-design`: Theory of Machines & Machine Design
- `thermal-fluids`: Thermal & Fluid Sciences
- `manufacturing-industrial`: Manufacturing & Industrial Systems
- `mechatronics-robotics`: Mechatronics & Robotics
- `energy-automotive`: Energy Conversion & Automotive

## Learning Levels
Each topic is mapped across a progression spectrum:
1. Discovery
2. Foundation
3. Beginner
4. Intermediate
5. Advanced
6. University
7. Professional
8. Research
9. Frontier

## Major Coverage Gaps
Based on the current inventory:
- Need more coverage in Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences.
- Need structured connections for biological and medical sciences.
- Need deeper mapping of prerequisites between disparate subjects (e.g., specific math requirements for electromagnetics).
